# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious visitors exploring exoplanets, planetary science, and the possibility of habitable worlds. They may arrive with little prior astronomy knowledge and use the site on a laptop or phone, often as a short, immersive discovery experience.

## Product Purpose

ORBITAL presents a cinematic field survey of Kepler-186f. It helps visitors build a vivid mental picture of the planet while distinguishing scientific context from artistic interpretation. Success means the visitor can explore the planet smoothly, understand what is known and uncertain, and leave with curiosity about exoplanets.

## Brand Personality

Quietly curious, precise, cinematic. The voice is measured and human, with a sense of scale and discovery. It avoids hype and treats unknown surface conditions honestly.

## Anti-references

Avoid game-like sci-fi HUDs, generic AI landing-page layouts, noisy dashboard telemetry, decorative technical labels, and unsupported claims presented as fact. Keep the experience focused on Kepler-186f; do not reintroduce other planet chapters or system selectors.

## Design Principles

- Let the planet and its changing terrain lead the experience.
- Make scientific context clear without interrupting exploration.
- Label artistic interpretation and uncertainty honestly.
- Use motion to explain scale, light, and surface detail, with a calm pace.
- Keep the single-planet journey legible and focused across screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support keyboard navigation, readable contrast, meaningful text alternatives for visual information, and reduced-motion preferences. Keep essential scientific context available without relying on color or animation alone.
